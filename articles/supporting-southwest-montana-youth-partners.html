Supporting Southwest Montana Youth Partners

Giving back does not always mean building something new. Sometimes it means volunteering the time and skills to help keep an important community resource running.

By Mephitideus Interactive · Published November 5, 2026

At Mephitideus Interactive, our work with technology extends beyond games. We have been helping Southwest Montana Youth Partners maintain its website on a volunteer basis, contributing our time and technical skills to benefit the community.

It is a practical way for a small studio to give back: support the people doing local work by helping with the website that connects them to the public.

A local mission worth supporting

Southwest Montana Youth Partners works to support access to quality, affordable youth programs in southwest Montana. On its website, the organization highlights the need for youth programs and childcare, and the pressures that a shortage of those services can place on families and the wider community.

That mission is the reason for our involvement. Our contribution is website maintenance, while Southwest Montana Youth Partners leads its own programs and community efforts. We are glad to play a supporting role.

Why website maintenance matters

A community organization's website is a place where people can learn about its mission, explore its initiatives, and find ways to get involved. Southwest Montana Youth Partners uses its site to share that information, along with news, contact details, and opportunities to support its work.

Keeping a website maintained is ongoing, behind-the-scenes work. Volunteering technical help is one way to support that work without making it another paid service the organization needs to commission from us.

Putting our skills to work for the community

Making games and helping maintain a community website are different kinds of projects, but both call for care, problem-solving, and attention to the people using them. This volunteer effort gives us a way to put those skills to use outside our own releases.

We believe contributing to the community can start with the skills you already have. For Mephitideus Interactive, that means lending a hand with technology so a local organization has support with its online presence.

Learn more and get involved

Visit the Southwest Montana Youth Partners website to learn about its mission, current initiatives, and ways to help. For questions about its programs or how to contribute, please contact the organization directly through its site.

We are grateful for the opportunity to help maintain this community resource and support the work Southwest Montana Youth Partners is doing.
