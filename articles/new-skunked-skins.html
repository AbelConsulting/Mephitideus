Five New Skins in Skunked: Way of the Spray

A fresh look for your next run, without a big price tag. The new $1.99 skins pack unlocks all five looks together.

By Mephitideus Interactive · Published November 5, 2026

Your next trip through Skunked: Way of the Spray can come with a change of wardrobe. Five new skins bring more personality to the ninja skunk, and you do not have to buy them one at a time: the whole skins package costs just $1.99.

Meet the new skins

The pack brings together five different looks for players who want to switch things up:

  • The Vampyr — for a run with a little vampire flair.
  • Kyle skunk — bring Kyle into your next skunk adventure.
  • Malodor skunk — choose a Malodor look for your next outing.
  • Pink box and dress skunk — add a splash of pink to the action.
  • Cyber skunk — take your skunk's style in a futuristic direction.

Whether you lean toward Vampyr, prefer the cyber look, or want something pink, the bundle means you can have the full lineup instead of choosing just one.

One small upgrade unlocks them all

The $1.99 price is for the entire skins pack, not for each individual skin. This optional upgrade unlocks all five together, making it an inexpensive extra for players who enjoy the game and want more ways to dress their skunk.

Skunked has no ads. Beyond the game purchase and this optional skins pack, there are no other in-game costs. The idea is simple: keep the game affordable and make the extra looks a small, worthwhile upgrade.

New to Way of the Spray?

Skunked is a 2D beat 'em up starring a ninja skunk, with combos, boss fights, and plenty of spray. Start with the free browser demo to get a feel for the action, or read our platform guide to compare the demo with the full campaign on Steam and Google Play.

Already playing? Pick a favorite from the new lineup and give your next run a fresh look. At $1.99 for all five skins, a wardrobe change does not need to be a big decision.
