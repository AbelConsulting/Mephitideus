How to Play Skunked: Browser Demo, Steam or Android?

Try the game in a browser first, or choose the platform for the complete campaign. Here is what each option includes.

By Mephitideus Interactive · Updated for the current release

Skunked: Way of the Spray is a fast 2D beat 'em up starring a ninja skunk. You can sample it in a web browser, then choose the full campaign on Steam or Google Play. The right option depends on whether you want a quick trial, a desktop game, or a touch-first mobile version.

Start with the free browser demo

The browser version is a focused demo of the opening two stages. It is the easiest way to try the movement, attacks, and boss pressure without installing the full game. Open the free web demo when you want a short session or want to see how the combat feels before choosing a platform.

The demo is a preview, not the complete campaign. If you are looking for all ten worlds and twenty stages, choose one of the full-game editions below.

Choose Steam for the desktop campaign

The Steam edition is the full game for Windows PC. Pick it if you prefer a desktop setup and want the complete campaign, Steam achievements, and the PC store experience. Check the current Steam listing for system requirements and compatibility before buying.

Choose Google Play for Android

The Google Play edition brings the full campaign to Android with touch controls. It suits shorter mobile sessions and players who want to continue away from a desktop. Review the current store listing for device compatibility and purchase details.
