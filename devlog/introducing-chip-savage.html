Introducing Chip Savage Golf: Underground Tour

An early look at a new arcade-golf action project from Mephitideus Interactive.

Mephitideus Interactive · September 30, 2026

We’re beginning to introduce Chip Savage Golf: Underground Tour, a golf-action game project in development. It is still a long way from release. We’re sharing it now to start building awareness while the game takes shape, not to suggest that it is finished or nearly ready.

What is the project?

The current concept follows Chip through outlaw courses, collecting Golden Cups and facing course champions on the way toward the final green. The browser build currently shows a Championship Tour, an Endless Back Nine, and a leaderboard. These are early-build features, and their form or scope may change during development.

What can you try today?

The existing browser build offers an early glimpse of the project. It is a work in progress, not a release announcement or a promise about when the finished game will be available.

Why share it this early?

We want players and people who follow independent games to know the project exists as it develops. For now, this page is an introduction and the browser build is a preview. We’ll share more when there is meaningful news to report.

For current project context, visit the Chip Savage project page. For media and approved studio artwork, see the press kit.
