About Mephitideus Interactive

Mephitideus Interactive is a small, independent studio focused on making games that are easy to pick up, hard to put down, and free of friction — no downloads, no accounts, no waiting. We build with the open web so anyone, anywhere, on any device can jump straight into the action.

Our first release, Skunked: Way of the Spray, set the tone: tight controls, responsive combat, and a healthy sense of humor. It’s the philosophy behind everything we make.
